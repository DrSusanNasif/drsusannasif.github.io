The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. To deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, Vercel processes technical data for each request, such as IP address, browser type and time of the request. Vercel acts as my data processor under its Data Processing Addendum, which includes the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers outside the European Economic Area. Vercel keeps these technical logs only for a short period.

Visit statistics, only with your consent

If you click Accept, the site counts your visit with Vercel Web Analytics. If you click Decline, or make no choice, nothing is counted.

  • No cookies are used. Visitors are recognised only by a temporary code derived from the request, which resets every day, so you cannot be followed across days or websites.
  • The statistics show pages visited, the referring website, country, browser, operating system and device type, in aggregated form.
  • Aggregated reports are kept for up to 12 months.
  • The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code).
  • Your choice is saved in your own browser only, so the site does not ask again. You can change it at any time with Privacy choices, also in the footer of every page.
