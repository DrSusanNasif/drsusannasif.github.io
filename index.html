Venture spotlight

Building the evidence
behind the decision.

STaiMIC is my initiative for reproducible, evidence-led biomedical computation, with a hands-on workflow training programme. It was selected to contribute to Domain 3, Digital & Technological Innovation, of the WHO/Europe Public Health Innovation Platform, and subsequently to two of its subgroups: SG2, Trustworthy AI Evidence Infrastructure for Public Health, and SG3, Clinical Decision Support & NCD Prevention.

Research and development continues in stealth. Partnership enquiries are welcome.

04 / Trajectory

A scientist’s rigour.
A storyteller’s instinct.

My career began with viruses and antiviral strategies. It grew into a wider question: how do we make scientific evidence understandable, useful and worthy of trust?

  1. 2005–14

    Research foundations

    KU Leuven and INSERM; doctoral research at the Rega Institute spanning hepatitis C and dengue antiviral strategies.

  2. 2014–20

    Visual science communication

    Founded Cimaza, translating medical evidence into illustration, animation and education.

  3. 2020–25

    Education across disciplines

    Independent biomedical education and communication; invited virology teaching at UCLA Extension.

  4. 2026–

    Translational leadership

    Founded STaiMIC to connect scientific strategy, reproducible computation and evidence governance.
