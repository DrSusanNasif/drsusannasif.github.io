Door 01 · Science communication

Stories that stay in memory.

A speech bubble holds only a few words, so every word must earn its place. Through storytelling and imagery, comics capture attention and carry key messages in a visual, memorable way, which matters most when research and policy are written in complex language.

Flagship · European Commission, DG SANTE · 2023

The Amazing Adventures of Syrgo and BC

26 languages

Published by the European Commission in all 24 official EU languages, plus Arabic and Ukrainian. Free to download.

Authored and led

I created the characters, storylines and initial panels, with artwork refined by Mar Bogdanis. Scientific review by ECDC and EMA.

Recommended, and part of an award-winning campaign

Listed by the Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia. The series was part of DG SANTE’s #UnitedInProtection campaign. In September 2023, the Forbes Italia Social Award was given to DG SANTE for that campaign.

Cimaza · KFAS · 2020

The Amazing Adventures of the Viruses

My award-winning comic, translated into Arabic by the Kuwait Foundation for the Advancement of Sciences in 2020 as The Amazing Adventures of the Virologists. The Arabic edition came with songs based on the comics, so children could learn while singing, and won the Arab Thinking Award.

03 Career

A scientist’s rigour. A storyteller’s instinct.

My career began with viruses and antiviral strategies. It grew into a wider question: how do we make scientific evidence understandable, useful and worthy of trust?

Feb 2026 to present

Matera, Italy, with international partners

  • Lead AI literacy and health literacy activities, including support for Cancer Literacy Day.
