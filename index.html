For children and families · European Commission, DG SANTE · 2023

The Amazing Adventures of Syrgo and BC

A three-volume vaccine-education comic series I authored for DG SANTE, published in all 24 official EU languages plus Arabic and Ukrainian. It was part of DG SANTE’s #UnitedInProtection campaign, which received the Forbes Italia Social Award.

For university and adult audiences · Rega Institute, KU Leuven

Virus characters for the Neyts lab

For the Laboratory of Virology and Antiviral Research, I sketched characters for each virus family, modelled in 3D by Mattia Genini and used by the lab to present its research. KU Leuven News featured the work in 2017.

About

Dr Susan Nasif Obeid‑Adorisio

I am a KU Leuven-trained virologist. After seven years in antiviral drug discovery, I founded the science communication studio Cimaza in 2014, authored the European Commission’s vaccine comics for DG SANTE, and in 2026 founded STaiMIC in Matera.
