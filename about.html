About

Dr Susan Nasif Obeid‑Adorisio, PhD

A KU Leuven-trained virologist and visual science communicator. After seven years in antiviral drug discovery at the Rega Institute, I founded the science communication studio Cimaza, authored the European Commission’s vaccine comics, and in 2026 founded STaiMIC, an initiative in its founding phase, in Matera.

Matera, Italy, with international partners

External Health Education Contractor

2020 to 2025

Independent Consultant, Health Education and Science Communication

Italy, Europe and MENA

Antiviral Drug Discovery Researcher
